🌐 ROUND 1 — THE OSINT INVESTIGATION (REAL-LIFE CASE FILES)
Skills: Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) • Digital Footprinting • Critical Thinking • Investigative Deduction • Fact Verification

Overview:
Round 1 is marketed and executed as an intensive OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) Qualifier Round. Teams are issued real-life, famous, and publicly available high-profile mystery case files (e.g., The Mysterious Death of Sushant Singh Rajput, The Death of Princess Diana, or other iconic declassified/unresolved cases).

The Mission:
1. Deep-Dive OSINT Reconnaissance:
   Teams must leverage the open web — including Google dorking, Wikipedia archives, news repositories, autopsy/police report leaks, flight paths, social footprints, and public statements.
2. Formulate a Conspiracy Theory / Investigative Thesis:
   Connect overlooked dots, uncover timeline discrepancies, cross-reference alibis, and construct a compelling, evidence-backed conspiracy theory or forensic hypothesis.
3. 10-Point Conclusion Report:
   Each team must submit a structured, 10-point investigative conclusion summarizing their core findings, key evidence links, timeline anomalies, and final deduction.

Evaluation & Scoring Rubric:
- Conclusions will be evaluated against strict multi-tier rubrics and cross-verified against expert pre-written case benchmarks and verified factual timelines.
- Points are awarded for:
  • Depth & authenticity of OSINT evidence uncovered (Primary Sources vs. Rumors)
  • Timeline consistency & contradiction identification
  • Quality, clarity, and logical rigor of the 10-Point Conclusion
  • Plausibility & forensic depth of the formulated theory

Progression to Grand Finale:
The highest-scoring investigative teams will qualify and advance to Round 2 — the real hands-on Cyber Crime & Digital Forensics CTF Challenge (Operation Nightfall).

Round 1 Flow:
Famous Real-Life Case File ➔ OSINT Deep-Dive (Google/Wiki/Archives) ➔ Evidence & Contradiction Mapping ➔ 10-Point Conclusion Submission ➔ Rubric Cross-Verification ➔ CTF Grand Finale Qualification
