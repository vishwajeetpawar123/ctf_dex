🔎 ROUND 1 — THE CASE FILE
Skills: Critical Thinking • OSINT • Problem Solving • Logical Reasoning

Teams will be given different mystery cases to investigate. They must analyze the available clues, connect evidence, identify possible motives, suspects, timelines, contradictions, and build their own conclusion/conspiracy theory.

Their conclusions will be evaluated using predefined case-solving metrics. Teams earning the highest scores will be shortlisted for Round 2 — the CTF.

Round 1 flow:
Case → Investigate → Analyze Clues → Build Theory → Score → Shortlist
